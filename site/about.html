Our story

Small moments, made well

We’re an independent cafe on NE Alberta Street in Portland. Our small team works with independent farms, thoughtful roasters, and local bakers who share our belief that the everyday things deserve a little attention.

That means a balanced espresso, a pastry still warm in the morning, and a friendly hello when you walk through the door. No fuss—just good things, made with care.
